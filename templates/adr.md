# ADR-<number>: <short decision title>

> An Architecture Decision Record (ADR) captures one important decision, its context and its
> consequences, so that people joining later understand why the system looks the way it does. Keep it
> to one page. Never edit an accepted ADR: write a new one that supersedes it.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | Proposed · Accepted · Deprecated · Superseded by ADR-<n> |
| Date | `<YYYY-MM-DD>` |
| Deciders | `<names>` |

## Context

*What forces are at play? Requirements, constraints, data, deadlines, numbers you measured. Example:
"BM25 reaches a document recall@5 of 0.917 on our evaluation set but fails on the Spanish-only
document."*

## Decision

*The decision in one or two sentences, in active voice: "We will use hybrid search (BM25 + embeddings
fused with RRF) for the support chatbot."*

## How we will know it was right

*A metric and a date to revisit, for example "re-check cross-lingual recall after one month of
