# Shipping

## Spain and Portugal

- Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 business days and costs €4.95.
- Standard delivery is **free for orders over €49**.
- Express delivery takes 1 business day and costs €6.95 (orders placed before 14:00).

## Rest of the European Union

Delivery takes 3 to 6 business days and costs €7.95.

## Latin America

We ship to some countries in Latin America. Delivery times and conditions are explained in our
Spanish-language help centre.

## Tracking your order

You get a tracking link by email when the parcel leaves our warehouse. You can also find it in the app
under Account > Orders.
