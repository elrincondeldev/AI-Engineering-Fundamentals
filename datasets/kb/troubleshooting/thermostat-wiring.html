Ember T3 wiring guide

Before you touch any wire, turn off the power to your heating system at the breaker.

Terminals

TerminalUsual colourPurpose
Rred24 V power
Cbluecommon wire: continuous power for the thermostat
W1whiteheating stage 1
Y1yellowcooling stage 1
Ggreenfan
O/Borangeheat pump reversing valve

No C-wire?

The Ember T3 needs continuous power from a C-wire. If your system has no C-wire, use the C-wire adapter kit (HW-ACC-CW, €19), which installs next to your boiler.

Wire colours are a convention, not a rule. Always check the terminal labels on your old thermostat.
