# Factory reset for every device

A factory reset deletes the device's Wi-Fi settings and removes it from your account. Use it when pairing keeps failing or before you return or give away a device.

## Ember T3

Press and hold the dial for 15 seconds until the screen shows RESET, then release.

## Glow A19

Switch the bulb off and on five times in a row; it blinks twice when reset.

## Spark Mini

Hold the side button for 10 seconds until the LED blinks red quickly.

## Watcher S1

Insert a paper clip into the pinhole for 5 seconds until the LED flashes blue.

## Lookout C2

Hold the reset button under the base for 10 seconds until you hear a chime.

## Hearth Hub

Hold the button on the back for 20 seconds until the ring light turns orange.
