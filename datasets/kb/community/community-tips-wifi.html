Community tips: stable Wi-Fi for smart bulbs

Posted by a community member

My Glow bulbs kept dropping off the network until I gave them their own 2.4 GHz network. Since then they have been rock solid.

Another tip: keep the router away from the microwave and do not hide it inside a metal cabinet.
