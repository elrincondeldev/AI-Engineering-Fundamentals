# Watcher S1 motion sensor

The Watcher S1 is a small battery-powered motion sensor. It triggers automations such as turning on the lights when someone walks into a room. It uses the low-power HomeMesh radio, so it needs a Hearth Hub.

## Key features

- 7 m detection range with a 120-degree field of view
- requires the Hearth Hub (it talks HomeMesh, not Wi-Fi)
- battery lasts about 18 months with default settings

## Setting it up

1. Make sure your Hearth Hub is online.
2. Pull the battery tab out of the Watcher S1; the LED flashes blue.
3. In the app, open the Hearth Hub and tap Add HomeMesh device.
4. Mount the sensor about 2 m high in the corner of the room.

## Resetting to factory settings

Insert a paper clip into the pinhole for 5 seconds until the LED flashes blue.
