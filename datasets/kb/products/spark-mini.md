# Spark Mini smart plug

The Spark Mini is a compact smart plug. Plug any appliance into it to switch it on and off from your phone, set schedules and see how much energy the appliance uses.

## Key features

- real-time energy monitoring in the app
- max load 3680 W (16 A at 230 V)
- schedules and away mode that randomises lights

## Setting it up

1. Plug the Spark Mini into a wall socket; the LED blinks blue.
2. Open the Hearthwise app, tap Add device and choose Spark Mini.
3. Connect the plug to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network when the app asks.

## Resetting to factory settings

Hold the side button for 10 seconds until the LED blinks red quickly.
