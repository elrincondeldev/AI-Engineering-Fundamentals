# Lookout C2 indoor camera

The Lookout C2 is the Hearthwise indoor security camera, launched on 1 September 2025. It records 2K video, sees in the dark and can save clips to a microSD card or, with Hearthwise Plus, to the cloud.

## Key features

- 2K video with night vision up to 8 m
- local recording to a microSD card of up to 256 GB
- cloud video history requires Hearthwise Plus
- first Hearthwise device that supports 5 GHz Wi-Fi

## Setting it up

1. Plug the camera into the included 5 V / 1 A USB-C adapter.
2. Open the Hearthwise app, tap Add device and choose Lookout C2.
3. Connect the camera to a 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz Wi-Fi network.
4. Optional: insert a microSD card of up to 256 GB for local recording.

## Resetting to factory settings

Hold the reset button under the base for 10 seconds until you hear a chime.
