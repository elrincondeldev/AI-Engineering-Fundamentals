# Glow A19 smart bulb

The Glow A19 is a colour smart bulb with a standard E27 base. It connects straight to your home Wi-Fi, so you do not need a hub, and you can control it from the app, with schedules or with automations.

## Key features

- 806 lumens (equivalent to a 60 W incandescent bulb)
- tunable white from 2700 K to 6500 K plus 16 million colours
- rated life of 25,000 hours

## Setting it up

1. Screw the bulb into an E27 socket and switch it on.
2. Open the Hearthwise app, tap Add device and choose Glow A19.
3. Connect the bulb to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network when the app asks.

## Resetting to factory settings

Switch the bulb off and on five times in a row; it blinks twice when reset.
