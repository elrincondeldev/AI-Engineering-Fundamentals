# Ember T3 smart thermostat

The Ember T3 is the Hearthwise smart thermostat. It replaces a standard wall thermostat, learns when you are usually at home and adjusts heating so that rooms are warm when you need them and cooler when nobody is there.

## Key features

- 3.5-inch colour touch display
- learns your schedule in about 7 days
- geofencing: lowers the temperature when everyone leaves
- works with HomeMesh 2.0 through the Hearth Hub

## Setting it up

1. Turn off the power to your heating system at the breaker.
2. Install the base plate and connect the wires (see the Ember T3 installation manual).
3. Attach the display, restore power and follow the on-screen setup wizard.
4. Open the Hearthwise app, tap Add device and scan the QR code on the screen.

## Resetting to factory settings

Press and hold the dial for 15 seconds until the screen shows RESET, then release.
