# Hearth Hub smart home hub

The Hearth Hub is the bridge between your HomeMesh devices and your network. It runs automations locally, so motion-triggered lights keep working even if your internet connection drops.

## Key features

- connects up to 64 HomeMesh devices
- runs automations locally, even when the internet is down
- Ethernet port for a more stable connection

## Setting it up

1. Connect the hub to power and, if you can, to your router with an Ethernet cable.
2. Open the Hearthwise app, tap Add device and choose Hearth Hub.
3. Wait until the ring light turns solid white, then add your HomeMesh devices.

## Resetting to factory settings

Hold the button on the back for 20 seconds until the ring light turns orange.
