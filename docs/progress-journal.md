# Progress journal

Copy this file into your fork (for example as `my-progress.md`) and fill it in as you go. Writing
down what you predicted, what you measured and what surprised you is the fastest way to turn a course
into skills you can talk about in an interview.

## How to use it

1. Before each lesson, write your **🤔 Predict first** answers in the module table.
2. After the lab, copy **one number you measured** and **one thing you broke on purpose**.
3. At the end of the module, score yourself on the module's rubric (1 to 4) and be honest: a 2 is a
   perfectly good place to be after one week.
4. Every four modules, fill in the reflection section.
